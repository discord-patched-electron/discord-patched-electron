# Vencord and Equicord

On your first launch, **Welcome!** offers Vencord and Equicord. Upgrading also
shows Welcome once if you have not completed it before and no mod is installed. Select **Install**
beside a mod, then select **Next** when you’re ready. You can also select **Next**
without installing a mod. Choose **Restart to finish** to open Discord, or
**Configure more** to replace Welcome with Settings and adjust your settings first.
Closing Settings finishes setup and restarts Discord automatically.
If finishing fails, Settings stays open so you can try again. Closing Welcome
finishes setup and launches Discord without requiring a mod.

To install, switch or remove a mod later, open **User Settings → DPE Settings →
Mods**. Only one mod can be active. Switching or removing a mod keeps its settings
and downloaded files. The active mod has an **Uninstall** button. Progress appears
below the choices, and changes take effect after restarting Discord.

Discord, Electron and package updates preserve your installed mod and its settings.
Both mods can still update themselves. An existing installation, including one in a
custom location, stays in place unless you choose a different mod.

If installation fails, check your connection and try again. The current mod stays
installed while its replacement downloads into a separate directory. Recently
verified downloads can be reused without downloading them again. If the service
reports a rate limit, wait for the indicated time before retrying.
Setup uses the official installers;
you do not need `sudo` or a terminal.

## Use an official installer manually

The [Vencord download page](https://vencord.dev/download/) and
[Equicord installation guide](https://docs.equicord.org/installing/) offer standalone
installers. Close Discord before using one. Choose **Canary** or **Custom Location**
and select your profile directory, normally `~/.config/discordcanary`.

To switch manually, uninstall the current mod with its installer first. Your mod
settings stay in place. See [custom profiles](linux-launcher.md#profiles) if you use
a different Discord profile directory.
